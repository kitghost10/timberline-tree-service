Tree work shouldn't leave your yard looking like a logging zone. Every Timberline crew follows a strict safety and property protection protocol.

01

An ISA Certified Arborist inspects canopy structure, power drop lines, and underground utilities, providing a locked-in flat-rate quote on the spot.

Arborist Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you should know before hiring a tree service company.

Are you fully licensed and insured with both General Liability and Workers' Comp?

Yes—100%. We carry a $2,000,000 General Liability policy and full Workers' Compensation insurance covering every climber and ground crew member on your property. We provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) with every written estimate so you never carry homeowner liability.

Will heavy tree equipment ruin my lawn or sprinkler system?

No. We deploy interlocking heavy-duty AlturnaMATS® ground protection mats across your grass and flag sprinkler heads prior to starting work. Our mats distribute weight evenly so even turf-sensitive yards remain rut-free.

Does tree removal automatically include stump grinding and debris haul-off?

Every tree removal includes 100% brush chipping, log haul-away, and thorough rake & blower cleanup. Because some homeowners prefer a flush-cut stump while others want deep below-grade grinding for new sod, we itemize stump grinding clearly on your quote (and you can toggle it right inside our Cost Calculator).

Do I need a city permit to remove a large tree, and do you handle it?

Protected or heritage-sized trees (typically 19"+ trunk diameter depending on municipality) or dead/hazardous trees near structures may require a city permit or arborist hazard affidavit. Our ISA Certified Arborists measure trunk DBH during your free estimate and handle all permit documentation on your behalf.
